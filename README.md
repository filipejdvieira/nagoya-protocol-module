# Before You Use That Strain: Nagoya Protocol Essentials for Microbial Strains

A short interactive e-learning module for researchers who receive microbial strains from other labs or collections. It explains when the EU rules implementing the Nagoya Protocol apply to a strain, and what you then have to do.

**▶ [Start the module](https://filipejdvieira.github.io/nagoya-protocol-module/)**  ·  About 15 minutes  ·  No login needed  ·  Works in any modern browser

---

## Who it is for

PhD students, postdocs, technicians and group leaders in microbiology and the life sciences in the EU who order, exchange or are sent strains, and anyone who supports them (research managers, compliance officers, collection staff).

## What you will learn

By the end of the module you should be able to:

- decide whether a strain falls under the EU Nagoya rules, by checking the access date, the provider country, exclusions and whether your work counts as utilisation;
- tell apart the documents you may receive: permit, internationally recognised certificate of compliance (IRCC), prior informed consent (PIC), mutually agreed terms (MAT) and material transfer agreement (MTA);
- carry out due diligence: seek, keep and pass on the right information;
- know when a due diligence declaration is due and where to submit it.

## How the module works

| Part | Slides | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | 1–10 | Narrated slides on the Protocol, users, due diligence and declarations. Blue info buttons give extra detail and links. |
| Case questions | 11–20 | Ten realistic scenarios ("Is this a Nagoya case for the receiving lab?") with feedback on every answer. |
| Wrap-up | 21 | Summary and a checklist to take back to the lab. |

The questions test the EU procedure, not the national law of each country. Where a case needs a country fact, it is shown on screen.

## Sources

- [Regulation (EU) No 511/2014](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/511/oj) on compliance measures for users from the Nagoya Protocol in the Union
- [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1866](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2015/1866/oj) (registered collections, best practices, monitoring)
- European Commission, [Access and benefit-sharing](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/nature-and-biodiversity/access-and-benefit-sharing_en): guidance on the scope and core obligations of the Regulation, and the DECLARE tool
- [ABS Clearing-House](https://absch.cbd.int/): country profiles, national measures and certificates of compliance

## Disclaimer

This module is an educational resource, not legal advice. Rules and national measures change: for a real case, check the ABS Clearing-House and contact your institution's compliance office or your national competent authority.
